We're Hiring

Marketing & Creative Associate

Abuja, Nigeria · Hybrid

About Us

Ché Orika Group operates two businesses.

Ché Orika Boutique

Women's ready-to-wear fashion. Dresses, sets, playsuits and statement pieces for women aged 20 to 35. We sell through our website, Instagram, TikTok, and our boutique in Gwarimpa, Abuja.

The Pattern House

Our B2B garment manufacturing arm. We produce for other fashion brands and designers across Nigeria. A separate brand with its own clients and identity.

This role covers marketing for both. The day-to-day focus is Ché Orika Boutique, but you will also help build The Pattern House's presence as it grows.

About the Role

As our Marketing & Creative Associate, you will be responsible for how the brand shows up online and in the market.

You will plan content, create it, post it, track what works, and adjust based on what the numbers tell you. You will also support operational marketing work including ad management, campaign planning, customer communication and coordination with the rest of the team.

This is a role where you will be involved in everything from coming up with the idea to filming it, editing it, writing the caption, posting it, and then looking at the data to figure out what to do next. You will have real ownership over the work and the space to bring your own ideas.

This role is hybrid. You will work from the boutique or on location at least two days per week, though some weeks may require more depending on shoot schedules or events. Outdoor content shoots across Abuja are a regular part of the job.

What it is like working here

We are a small, close team building two businesses at the same time. The work is fast-paced and the standards are high, but the environment is supportive. You will work directly with the founder, your ideas will be heard, and when something you create does well, everyone celebrates it.

We care about doing good work and we care about the people doing it. We give honest feedback because we want everyone to get better, not because we want to find fault. If you are the kind of person who enjoys being part of something early, having a say in how things are built, and seeing the direct impact of your work, you will enjoy it here.

Key Responsibilities

Strategy and planning

  • Own and maintain the weekly content calendar.
  • Understand who our customer is, what she responds to, and what drives her to buy or enquire.
  • Research trends and competitor activity, and bring ideas for what we could try.
  • Plan content around product launches, promotions, seasonal moments and business goals.
  • Use analytics to inform decisions and share learnings with the team.

Content creation

  • Shoot and edit short-form video content (Instagram Reels, TikTok, Stories).
  • Style outfits, direct models and choose shoot locations.
  • Write captions that communicate value and encourage action.
  • Ensure every product post includes the price and a clear call to action.
  • Create content that speaks to different audiences and channels as the brand grows.

Community and customer engagement

  • Respond to DMs and comments within 30 minutes during business hours.
  • Turn conversations into warm leads for the sales team.
  • Engage with followers, potential customers and the wider brand community daily.

Ads and performance

  • Set up and manage ad campaigns on Meta (Instagram and Facebook).
  • Monitor ad performance and flag anything that needs attention early.
  • Maintain the content tracker with accurate, up-to-date records.

Operational support

  • Coordinate with the founder on brand direction, new product drops and shoot planning.
  • Support the preparation and execution of campaigns, events and collaborations.
  • Keep brand assets, content files and documentation organised and accessible.

What We Are Looking For

We are looking for someone creative, self-directed and genuinely interested in building something.

  • Experience creating content for a fashion, lifestyle or consumer brand. Personal brand work counts if the quality and results are there.
  • Strong video skills. You can shoot on a phone, edit in CapCut or similar tools, and produce content that feels intentional and well put together.
  • A working understanding of analytics. You can look at post performance and explain what happened and what to try next.
  • The ability to write clearly, whether that is captions, briefs, DM responses or ad copy.
  • Comfortable adapting your content style for different platforms and audiences.
  • Familiarity with Meta Ads Manager or a willingness to learn it quickly.
  • A base in Abuja and availability to work from the boutique or on location at least two days per week.
Nice to Have
  • Experience running paid ad campaigns and interpreting the results.
  • Familiarity with content planning tools and social media scheduling platforms.
  • Experience working in a small team where you wear multiple hats.
  • An eye for styling and visual composition.

What Success Looks Like

  • Content is planned ahead of time and posted consistently.
  • Every product post includes a price, a clear call to action, and is trackable.
  • You can show, with data, which content types are driving the most engagement, DMs and sales.
  • DM response times stay under 30 minutes during business hours.
  • Ad campaigns are monitored regularly and adjusted when something is not performing.
  • The brand has a visible, consistent online presence across all platforms.
  • Content quality improves month over month as you learn from performance data.
  • The founder and team trust your judgement and feel confident giving you room to run with ideas.
